Research projects

EyeMotion: Eye-Gaze Proportional ControlUniversity of New Haven
  • Built a webcam-based interface (Python, MediaPipe, OpenCV) that maps iris position to a proportional control signal, removing the need for dedicated eye-tracking hardware.
  • Evaluated it on a ball-and-beam task, an unstable control benchmark; manuscript under review.
NeuroGrip: EMG-Controlled Robotic HandIndependent project
  • Controlled an 11-degree-of-freedom, 3D-printed robotic hand in real time from 8-channel forearm EMG with a neural network trained in PyTorch, driving tendon pulleys and knuckle servos through an Arduino.
  • Reduced output flicker with a temporal-consistency term in the training loss; built a kinematic simulator from the CAD assembly to check joint ranges.
GrapGrow: Modular Pediatric Prosthetic ArmWith Yale University students, Fall 2026 – present
  • Developing a 3D-printable prosthetic arm sized from anthropometric data for ages 8–14, with a socket, an extension mechanism that grows with the child, and a quick attach/detach interface for accessories.
Rehabilitation platformInstrumentation and Sensors Laboratory
  • Adapting a ball-and-beam control platform into a rehabilitation tool, with training strategies for stroke patients with mobility impairments.
